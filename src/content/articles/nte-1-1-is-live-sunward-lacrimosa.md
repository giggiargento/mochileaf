Maintenance ended and **Version 1.1 "Dreamwalk Corridor"** is officially playable. Hotta Studio and Perfect World Games rolled the update out on **June 3, 2026** after a scheduled window (06:00–11:00 UTC+8), with **Annulith ×600** compensation waiting in mail for eligible accounts.

If you stepped away after launch week, this is the calm catch-up: what actually opened, what is time-limited, and what can wait.

## Sunward Island is the headline

For the first time, the main story steps **outside Hethereau**. The episode **Dreamwalk Corridor** unlocks **Sunward Island** — a coastal zone built around an anomaly charity house, with beaches, harbor towns, forests, and the new location anomaly **Corridor of Echoes**.

Official patch notes describe it as "not your usual tourist location," which tracks with the purple fog, wrecked ships, and statue-lined fights shown in preview streams. It feels like a summer holiday that keeps turning surreal — very NTE.

## Lacrimosa banner: now through June 24

**Lacrimosa** (Chaos / Liquid Arc) is the first limited S-class of 1.1 on the **Fading Reverie** board, live from maintenance through **June 24, 05:59 UTC+8**. The paired arc banner **Nocturne Special** features **The Last Rose** on the same schedule.

**Chaos** (Lakshana) takes over on **June 24** with the **Forsaken Path** board running until **July 8** — the two banners do not overlap, so plan pulls accordingly.

## Events worth marking on your calendar

Several 1.1 events share a long window (**June 3 – July 8**), including **Sunward Travelogue**, **What's Baking**, **Underground Circuit** (six-player vehicular brawl), and **The Long Dream** (world encounters with Lacrimosa).

Shorter windows to note:

- **Fons Rush** — June 8–15 (double hobby Fons up to a stamina cap; handy if you are saving for the Porsche mystery boxes)  
- **Hunter's Crucible** — June 18 – July 8  
- **Beyond the Rails** cycle reset — June 4 for the new Prime Circle window  

## Porsche collab and city upgrades

The **Everdriving** mystery box event offers the **Porsche 918 Spyder**, collab outfit **Perfect Partners**, and related cosmetics. **Pegasus Residence** also arrived as a new property at City Tycoon level 13.

Quality-of-life touches in the same patch include **auto-drive**, phone customization, a **mute Taygedo** toggle during story scenes, and mobile performance tuning — small comforts that add up during a long exploration session.

## A gentle way back in

1. Claim maintenance mail, then restart the client so assets load cleanly.  
2. Resume main story at **A Research in Scarlet** if you are not on Sunward yet.  
3. Pick **one** goal for the week: story island tour, Lacrimosa banner, or Porsche savings — not all three on day one.

We will keep Mochileaf's Lacrimosa notes and team ideas updated as the community settles into Sunward Island.
