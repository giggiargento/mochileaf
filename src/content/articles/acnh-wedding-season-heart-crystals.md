**Wedding Season** is Animal Crossing's gentle June marathon — same dates every year, **June 1–30**, both hemispheres. Reese and Cyrus need help styling anniversary shoots on **Harv's Island**, and you earn **Heart Crystals** to trade for wedding furniture.

No patch required in 2026 beyond keeping your game updated; this is calendar content, not a download day.

## Daily loop in plain language

1. Answer Harvey's call (or fly to Harv's Island after Isabelle's announcement).  
2. Style the room Reese describes — ceremony, reception, or party themes unlock over the month.  
3. Photograph the couple; Reese awards **Heart Crystals** and sometimes a wedding-series piece.  
4. Trade crystals with **Cyrus** for catalog items (up to **eight purchases per day**).  

Extra items land in your **mailbox** if inventory is full — check there before assuming you lost a reward.

## How to earn more crystals (without stress)

Reese reacts to how well the room matches her prompt. Community guides generally report:

- **Days 1–6:** roughly **4–14** crystals per shoot depending on decor  
- **Day 7 onward:** higher daily ceiling (up to about **18** once party setups unlock)  

Practical tips that stay cozy:

- Place **multiple** wedding items when she asks for elegance — one lonely arch rarely impresses.  
- Follow her color hints; custom designs everywhere can dilute the theme.  
- Repeat the shoot daily even if you only earn the minimum — June has **30** days, not 30 perfect rooms.

## What to buy first from Cyrus

Prioritize items that are **hard to substitute** in builds you actually want:

- **Wedding bench + table** for outdoor ceremonies  
- **Flower stands** if you photograph islands often  
- **Wand recipe** if you wear coordinated outfits  

Skip buying every color variant in June — crystals are finite, and the catalog returns next year.

## Nook Shopping extras

Through June, **Able Sisters** and **Nook Shopping** sell complementary pieces (shiromuku sets, nuptial bells, ring pillows). These cost Bells, not crystals — nice if you are short on Heart Crystals but want the vibe.

## Pair with Bug-Off prep (NH)

Northern Hemisphere **Bug-Off** lands **June 28**. If you are doing daily wedding runs all month, set aside one evening to plant flowers near the plaza — Flick pays **1.5×** for bugs on event day.

## When to stop

You do not need every Cyrus item to enjoy Wedding Season. Pick a small decor goal — beach chapel, garden reception, or one room in your house — and call it done when that vision feels complete.
