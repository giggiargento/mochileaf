Animal Crossing: New Horizons is not getting a fresh version patch in **June 2026** — the latest game update remains **3.0.3** from late April. That does not mean the month is quiet.

June is one of the busiest **calendar** months on the island: **Wedding Season** runs the full month, summer critters rotate in (or out), and Northern Hemisphere players get **Flick's Bug-Off** before July arrives.

## Wedding Season — all of June

From **June 1 through June 30**, Reese and Cyrus return to **Harv's Island** for daily wedding photo shoots. Isabelle announces the season on the first day; Harvey calls you in to help style chapel setups in Photopia.

Decorate the room with wedding furniture, snap a photo, and earn **Heart Crystals** plus wedding-series items. Crystals trade with Cyrus for more decor — up to eight purchases per day. Better styling generally means more crystals, so treat it like a gentle daily creative prompt, not a min-max race.

**Able Sisters** and **Nook Shopping** also rotate wedding-themed clothing and small items through June (shiromuku pieces, nuptial bells, ring pillows, and similar pieces depending on your catalog progress).

Both hemispheres share the same Wedding Season dates — no north/south split for this event.

## Bug-Off — June 28 (Northern Hemisphere)

While Wedding Season is a month-long mood, **Flick's Bug-Off** is a single-day sprint: **Saturday, June 28, 2026** from **9:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m.** local time on Northern Hemisphere islands.

Talk to Flick in the plaza to enter three-minute catching rounds. Your first entry is free; repeats cost **500 Bells**. Every **10 points** earns a prize item, and Flick buys bugs at **1.5×** value on event day — a nice bonus if your storage is overflowing.

Southern Hemisphere players wait until their winter Bug-Off months (November–February cycle); June is their tarantula-and-sea-creature season instead.

## Also on the June calendar

Community guides for June 2026 highlight a few overlapping Nook Shopping moments worth peeking at between wedding runs:

- **Dragon Boat & Dano Festival** items — mid-June  
- **Summer / Winter Solstice** decor — June 15–21 depending on hemisphere  
- **Father's Day** and **International Children's Day** themed pieces — various June windows  

Critter lists shift too: Northern Hemisphere summer brings sharks and river fish like Dorado and Arowana; Southern Hemisphere winter players should watch sea creatures and fish leaving after the month ends.

## A low-stress June plan

- **Daily:** one Wedding Season shoot if you are chasing Heart Crystals for a specific piece.  
- **Weekly:** check Nook Shopping for solstice or festival items you like.  
- **June 28 (NH):** stack nets, clear flowers near the plaza, and treat Bug-Off as a social afternoon — not a perfection sprint.

If you are still on an older save, remember Wedding Season requires **version 2.0+** — and the January **3.0** resort update is worth installing first if you have been away since 2022.
