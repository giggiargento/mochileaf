Waiting for **update 1.7** is the perfect excuse to start a fresh save — or finally finish the Community Center on a long-abandoned farm. Stardew in **2026** still runs on **1.6** mechanics, and the cozy win is picking a pace you can sustain past Spring 14.

## Choose a farm that matches your fantasy

Every 1.6 layout nudges a different skill:

| Farm | Best if you want… |
|------|-------------------|
| **Standard** | Open fields, simplest mental map |
| **Meadowlands** | Chickens day one, cozy livestock vibes |
| **Forest** | Forage and hardwood without long walks |
| **Riverland** | Fishing focus plus a free fish smoker |
| **Hill-top** | Mining quarry on the property |
| **Beach** | Challenge run (limited sprinkler space) |
| **Four Corners** | Organized quadrants — great for multiplayer |
| **Wilderness** | Combat XP at night (toggle monsters off if that stresses you) |

If you are returning after years away, **Standard** or **Meadowlands** keeps cognitive load low. Save Beach or Wilderness for a second playthrough.

## Spring crop route (low stress)

You do not need min-max spreadsheets for a satisfying year one:

1. **Days 1–12:** Parsnips or potatoes for quick cash and farming XP.  
2. **Egg Festival (13th):** Buy **strawberry seeds** and plant immediately — they regrow all Spring.  
3. **Late Spring:** Kale if you want one more harvest before Summer.  

In Summer, **blueberries** are the forgiving money crop; in Fall, **cranberries** repeat-harvest without much babysitting. Our [year-one route](/stardew-valley/guides/stardew-valley-year-one-guide) goes deeper if you want a full seasonal checklist.

## One main side goal

Pick **one** non-farming focus for your first season:

- **Community Center** bundles (read the bulletin early, stash one of each forage)  
- **Mine to floor 40** before Summer for better geodes  
- **Two friendships** you genuinely like talking to  

Switching all three every day is how Stardew starts feeling like homework.

## Machines and upgrades

Rainy days are for **watering can** upgrades at the blacksmith — never on a day you need to water crops. Unlock **preserves jars** or a **mayonnaise machine** before you build five different processors; one finished good beats three half-empty sheds.

Check the **TV** (Queen of Sauce, fortune teller) when you pass the farmhouse — free recipes and mine hints add up quietly.

## Burnout guardrails

- Stop when energy hits red; eat foraged snacks instead of buying salads every night.  
- Skip a in-game day if real life is heavy — crops wilt, friendships wait.  
- Do not compare your farm to screenshot tours; decorate when inspiration strikes, not by day three.

Clint and Sandy romance routes are coming in **1.7**, but Pelican Town already has years of stories worth your time. Play 2026 like a long novel, not a race to the credits.
