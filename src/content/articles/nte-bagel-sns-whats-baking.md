If you have been scrolling **Bagel** since launch week, you already know the vibe — NPC hunters, city gossip, and the occasional surreal anomaly screenshot mixed into your feed.

**Version 1.1** changes the contract. Bagel is no longer something you only read. It is a **creator platform** inside Hethereau, and the **What's Baking** event (June 3–July 8) rewards you for treating it that way — without turning your evenings into an engagement grind.

This is our dedicated look at Bagel only. For Sunward Island routes or Lacrimosa banner math, see our [Sunward Island cozy guide](/neverness-to-everness/guides/nte-sunward-island-cozy-guide) instead.

## What Bagel SNS actually is now

Before 1.1, Bagel functioned like ambient world-building — flavor text from hunters and locals. After the **Dreamwalk Corridor** update, Appraisers can:

- Publish posts built from **in-game album photos and video clips**
- Like, follow, and bookmark other players' moments
- Track **views, likes, and bookmarks** in the **Creator Hub** (stats refresh daily at server midnight)

Official patch notes also clarify what Bagel is *not*: fan art and external uploads belong on **Taygedo** community channels for now. Bagel stays inside the game's own screenshot economy — which keeps the feed visually consistent and avoids moderation surprises.

## Praise Jam and the Bagel Shop

Engagement converts into **Praise Jam** through the **Creator Incentive Program**. Spend it at the **Bagel Shop** on practical rewards — official event copy lists items such as **Annuliths**, **Fabricated Dice**, and **Fons**, depending on the current rotation.

You do not need viral posts to participate. A steady trickle of honest city photos often beats one over-edited montage followed by silence.

## What's Baking — the 1.1 creator event

**What's Baking** runs the full **June 3–July 8** window alongside other 1.1 events. Think of it as Hotta Studio nudging you to **become a Bagel creator** for the summer patch cycle.

A cozy weekly rhythm that worked for our team:

| Day | Bagel habit |
| --- | --- |
| Weekday | One screenshot after dailies or a commission — plaza, apartment, or Sunward sunset |
| Weekend | Short clip if you hit Underground Circuit or Fight Club — motion reads well in feed |
| Any day | Check Creator Hub once, claim Praise Jam, ignore rank anxiety |

## Side systems that pair well with Bagel

Bagel posts shine when you already have something worth framing:

- **Chiz's new life-skill highlights** — Pink Paws Heist collectibles make colorful stills  
- **Pegasus Residence** interiors — property tours perform well without spoilers  
- **Underground Circuit** — chaotic racing clips are naturally shareable  

None of these are required. They simply give you subjects beyond standing in the plaza repeating the same emote.

## Gentle creator rules

- **Album first** — compose in photo mode, then post; do not reverse the workflow and stress about captions.  
- **One goal per week** — creator rewards *or* banner pulls *or* Porsche savings, not all three.  
- **Mute Taygedo** if story scenes distract you — 1.1 added that toggle specifically because city life and narrative overlap more now.

Bagel is optional flavor in a game that already asks plenty of your attention. Treat it like a cozy journal of Hethereau, not a second job.
